01 — About

I'm a Chartered Accountancy aspirant who thinks in numbers but builds in code. Outside of my CA training, I design and ship web platforms, product ideas, and AI-assisted tools — drawn to problems in fintech, real estate, and India's blue-collar workforce, where a finance brain and a product mindset turn out to be the same skill.

Background

Currently training as a Chartered Accountant, building a foundation in finance, audit, and business fundamentals — while running product and design work alongside it.

Approach

I treat every product like a finance problem first: understand the real constraint, then design and build the simplest thing that solves it — with AI-assisted workflows to move faster without cutting corners.

Interests

AI-assisted product workflows, fintech, real estate technology, and marketplaces built for India's blue-collar workforce.
